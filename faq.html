Customer Service

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about shopping with Wear with Modern. Contact us if you need help with a specific order.

Where does Wear with Modern deliver?

The storefront currently lists Pakistan-wide delivery. Confirm delivery availability for your address, the estimated timing and any applicable charges with our team.

Which payment method is available?

Cash on Delivery is listed on the storefront. Confirm the final payable amount and delivery arrangements before placing an order.

How do I confirm an order?

The current website form saves order details in the browser and does not automatically send them to the store. Contact us directly by phone, email or WhatsApp to arrange and confirm an order.

Can I return or exchange an item?

The store advertises a 7-day return policy. Contact us within 7 days of receiving your item. See Return & Exchange for the request steps and details that need to be confirmed.

How can I ask about a product?

Contact us with the product name and any relevant size or colour details. We can confirm current availability and product information.
