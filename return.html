Customer Service

Return & Exchange

We want you to feel confident about your purchase. Contact us promptly if something is not right.

7-day return window

Our storefront currently advertises a 7-day return policy. If you need a return or exchange, contact us within 7 days of receiving your item so we can review your request.

How to request help

  1. Contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp within the 7-day window.
  2. Share your name, order details and a clear description of the issue. Photos can help us understand a product concern.
  3. Wait for our reply and instructions before sending an item back.

Exchanges and next steps

Exchange options depend on product availability. We will discuss the available resolution and explain any applicable return or delivery arrangements with you before you proceed.

Please keep the item and its packaging until you have received instructions. Do not send an item to an address that has not been confirmed by our team.

Please note: The website does not publish detailed eligibility rules, return-shipping charges or refund timelines. Contact us to confirm the process for your specific request.
