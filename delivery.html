Delivery Information

The key details to check before arranging delivery with Wear with Modern.

Delivery area

Wear with Modern currently lists Pakistan-wide delivery. Availability for a particular city or address should be confirmed with our team before an order is finalized.

Delivery times and charges

Delivery estimates and any applicable charges can vary by destination and courier availability. The store does not publish a fixed delivery timeframe or shipping rate on this page, so please confirm both with us when arranging your order.

Cash on delivery

Cash on Delivery is listed as a payment option on our storefront. Please confirm the payable total and delivery arrangements with our team before dispatch.

Need help?

Contact us with your name, phone number, delivery city and order details. Please do not include sensitive payment information in your message.

Ordering note: The current website form stores submitted order details in the browser and does not automatically send them to the store. Contact us directly to confirm an order and delivery.
