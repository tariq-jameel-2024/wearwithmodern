Privacy Policy

This page explains what information the current Wear with Modern storefront asks for and how the website handles it.

Information entered on the website

The order form can ask for your name, phone number, delivery address, city, province or region, product and quantity details, and delivery notes. Email and some address fields are optional.

How the current order form works

At present, the storefront saves order records in browser storage on the device and browser profile used to place an order. The form does not automatically send the order to Wear with Modern or a delivery company. Anyone with access to that browser profile may be able to access information stored there.

The website also stores a local theme preference in your browser. Clearing browser site data may remove locally saved orders and preferences.

Payments and third-party resources

The current order form does not request card or bank details. Cash on Delivery is shown as a store payment option. Do not send sensitive payment information by email or messaging.

These pages load font files from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those files, the provider may receive standard technical request information such as your IP address and browser details.

Questions about your information

Because the current order flow is stored in your browser, the store may not receive a copy of an order submitted through that form. Contact us directly if you need to arrange an order or ask a privacy question.

Important: Do not use a shared or public browser to enter personal delivery details. This policy describes the website's current behavior and should be reviewed if the order system changes.
